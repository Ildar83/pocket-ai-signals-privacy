Privacy Policy — Pocket AI Signals

Policy date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Pocket AI Signals, listed in the Android store as Pocket AISignals (package name: com.pocketai.signals).

The app is developed by Σpsilon AI. The developer is Ильдар Хузин (Ildar Khuzin). For privacy questions or requests, contact otrade2019@gmail.com.

1. App use and information stored on your device

The app’s core features do not require registration. Its core interface does not ask you to enter a name, phone number, or email address.

The app’s core content and content history are stored locally on your device. This content history is not a record of your personal trades or financial transactions.

The app also stores local preferences, including:

  • Whether you have completed onboarding.
  • Your notification setting.

The notification toggle saves a local preference only. It does not register the device for push notifications or enable notification delivery.

The core app does not include a separate user screen-analytics feature. Information handled by the advertising SDK is described below.

2. Advertising and Yandex Mobile Ads

The app displays banner and interstitial advertisements using Yandex Mobile Ads SDK, version 8.2.0. The advertising SDK may initialize shortly after the app launches or earlier in response to an action in the app. Initialization is not conditional on accepting this policy or completing an in-app consent screen.

According to Yandex’s documentation for SDK version 8, the SDK collects device or other identifiers, including the advertising ID, and shares them with Yandex by default when the appropriate permission is present. Yandex describes the purposes as advertising and marketing, including displaying and targeting advertisements and measuring advertising performance.

The app does not explicitly provide your name, email address, phone number, gender, age, financial details, or geographic coordinates in its advertising requests. Yandex’s SDK version 8 documentation lists location, app interactions, and crash logs as not collected by default. These statements concern the app’s advertising requests and the SDK’s documented default behavior; they do not describe every activity of Yandex outside this app.

Yandex handles information it receives under its own privacy policy and service practices. For further information, see:

  • Yandex Mobile Ads Android privacy documentation
  • Yandex Privacy Policy
  • Yandex Ads Privacy Policy

Depending on your Android version and device, your system settings may offer controls to reset or delete the advertising ID or limit its use. These controls do not necessarily stop all advertisements from appearing or prevent every form of information processing by an advertising provider.

3. Emails and privacy requests

If you contact otrade2019@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, the contents of your message, and any information or attachments you choose to provide. This information is used to respond to your inquiry, investigate the issue you report, or handle your privacy request. Correspondence is also handled by the email service providers used to send and receive your message.

Please do not send passwords, sensitive financial information, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

4. Retention and deletion

Local app content and preferences remain on your device until they are removed or replaced. You can remove locally stored app data through Android’s app settings by choosing the option to clear the app’s storage or data. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local app data. These actions do not delete information already held by Yandex or information you have sent to the developer by email.

The app does not provide an in-app deletion button or an integrated mechanism for deleting data held by Yandex.

Support emails are retained only as long as necessary to respond to and resolve the request or fulfill applicable legal obligations.

Information held by Yandex is subject to Yandex’s retention and deletion practices. The developer does not control Yandex’s retention periods and cannot promise automatic, immediate, or guaranteed deletion of information held by Yandex.

5. Security

The Yandex advertising SDK uses HTTPS/TLS to protect information during transmission. Local app information is stored within the app’s storage on your device. The security of that information also depends on your device and its settings.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage local app data through Android settings and use the advertising ID controls available on your device.

You may email otrade2019@gmail.com to ask about information under the developer’s control or to request access, correction, or deletion. Requests are handled in accordance with applicable law. The developer may ask for reasonable information needed to identify the relevant correspondence or understand the request. Please do not provide passwords or sensitive financial information for identification.

A request to the developer concerns information the developer controls, such as correspondence you have sent. For information held independently by Yandex, consult Yandex’s privacy policy and its available privacy-request channels. The developer cannot directly delete Yandex-held information through the app.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app or its information-handling practices. An updated version will show its policy date at the top of the page.

This standalone privacy-policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The page itself contains no advertising, analytics scripts, registration forms, or developer-set tracking cookies. This does not mean that the Android app has no advertisements or that its advertising SDK processes no information.

GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. Hosting-related processing is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
